Privacy Policy

Effective August 8, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how Diplomatia collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you access the game. By using Diplomatia, you acknowledge this policy.

Information collected

  • Account information, including your email address and securely hashed password.
  • Game information, including your nation, leader, settings, actions, messages, Alliances, market activity, conflicts, and progress.
  • Technical and activity information, such as session identifiers, login and activity times, browser or device information, IP address, and server logs.
  • Communications you send through official Diplomatia support channels.

Do not submit sensitive personal information through nation names, profiles, messages, or other public game fields.

How information is used

Diplomatia uses information to create and secure accounts, operate game systems, display public nation information, prevent abuse, investigate errors, enforce the Terms of Service, communicate service notices, and improve the game.

Public information

Nation and leader names, government type, continent, user type, Alliance affiliation, activity status, nation age, Guardian status, economic Gear, Province setup, and other clearly public game information may be visible to other players. Email addresses, password hashes, treasury, private stockpiles, and private production information are not intended to be public.

Cookies and local storage

Diplomatia uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in and may use local browser storage for preferences such as theme. Disabling these technologies may prevent the game from working correctly.

Sharing

Information may be processed by hosting, database, security, and infrastructure providers as necessary to run Diplomatia. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or Diplomatia, investigate fraud or abuse, or complete a reorganization or transfer of the service. Diplomatia does not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Information is retained while needed to operate Diplomatia, maintain game and security records, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security or uninterrupted availability.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information through an official support channel made available by Diplomatia. Requests may require identity verification and may be limited where retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate game records. Applicable law may provide additional rights and a right to contact a local data-protection authority.

International processing and minors

Information may be processed in countries other than your own, where protections may differ. Diplomatia is not directed to children under 13. Do not use Diplomatia if local law does not permit you to consent to its data practices.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Diplomatia changes. Material updates may be announced in-game. Questions or privacy requests may be submitted through an official Diplomatia support channel.
