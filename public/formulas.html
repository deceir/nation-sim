These are the principal formulas used by Diplomatia. Displayed values are rounded where appropriate, and named Gear, policy, project, building, distress, and event modifiers apply where shown in-game.

Power Level (PL)

PL = round(500 × Provinces) + round(25 × √Total Infrastructure) + round(300 × Completed Projects) + round(Military)Military = 0.02 × Soldiers + 2 × Tanks + 10 × Ships + 6 × Fighter Jets + 3 × Drones

Completed starter projects and completed long-term National Projects both count. Projects still under construction do not. Military equipment listed for sale remains part of its nation’s PL until ownership changes. The square-root Infrastructure term prevents unlimited Infrastructure from dominating the ranking.

Citizen economy

Effective Infrastructure

At 100 or below: E = Infrastructure
Above 100: E = 100 + 800 × ln(1 + (Infrastructure − 100) / 800)

Infrastructure continues to support more population, but each additional point contributes less than the last after 100.

Supported population

Supported Population = max(1,000, E × 90 × Agriculture × Civil × Education × Satisfaction × Disease × Crime × Density)Population Capacity = Supported Population × 1.20

Citizen income and tax

Citizen Income = 500 × Happiness modifier × Education modifier × Technology modifierDaily Tax = Effective Population × Citizen Income × Tax Rate × Commerce × Doctrine × Commercial Upgrade × Employment × Tax Collection

Employment is measured against a 72% baseline. Civic Institutions, local upgrades, policies, power supply, disease, crime, Gear, and projects alter the named terms.

GDP

GDP = Projected Daily Citizen Tax × 365

GDP currently values citizen tax income only. Resources and stockpiles are excluded until market pricing is mature enough to value them consistently.

Infrastructure and Provinces

Infrastructure purchase

Cost per point = [475 + max(0, Current Infrastructure − 5)^2.15 / 760] × 100 × Technology DiscountTechnology Discount = max(0.72, 1 − 0.004 × Technology)

Bulk purchases charge each point along the curve. Infrastructure upkeep is 32 per point per day, with an additional 12% step for every 1,200 Infrastructure in the same Province, before policy and project modifiers.

Province upgrade capacity

Upgrade Capacity = 5 + floor(max(0, Infrastructure − 100) / 50)

Infrastructure does not consume an upgrade slot. Every 50 Infrastructure above 100 opens another slot.

Province upgrade cost

Cost = Base Cost × 80 × (Next Level)^1.72 × [1 + 0.08 × (Infrastructure / 1,000)]

Levels 12–15 receive an additional steep cost multiplier. Upgrade effects begin diminishing after level 8, and level 15 is the hard cap.

Founding a Province

Cash = ¥25,000,000 × Current Provinces^2.55 × Gear × PolicyConstruction Materials = ceil(40 × Current Provinces^2.15)

The normal seven-day founding cooldown applies separately.

Population and food

Hourly population growth

Growth = Effective Population × 0.002 × (0.45 + Happiness / 100) × (0.85 + Education / 500) × National Modifiers ÷ 24 × Variance

Variance is 0.90–1.10. Growth cannot exceed remaining population capacity. Nations below their combined ceiling receive at least 5–20 citizens per turn.

Daily civilian food demand

Food = Population × 0.00065 × [1 + 0.45 × (Population / 100,000)^0.35]

Social policies can modify the result. Soldiers consume 0.0025 Foodstuffs each day and additional food while fighting.

National Projects

Standard Project Slots = 3 + floor(Total Infrastructure / 1,000) + floor(max(0, Provinces − 1) / 2)

Economic Specializations and National Economy projects use standard slots. Military Industry projects use separate capacity and do not consume these slots.

Luxury consumption

Size Efficiency = clamp(Population / 2,000,000 + Provinces / 10, 0.25, 2.25)Daily Cap = 20 + Population × 0.00004 + Provinces × 12Income = Luxury Goods Consumed × ¥35 × Size Efficiency

The Luxury Market Authority project is required before consumption can be enabled.

Trade delivery

Turns = ceil([3 + 30 × (Distance Modifier − 1)^3] × Shipment Size Modifier)Shipping Fee = Trade Value × [0.75% + 3% × (Distance Modifier − 1)^1.30]Delay Risk = 0.5% + 4% × (Distance Modifier − 1)

The regional distance table supplies the Distance Modifier. Loads above 1,000 tonnes use a 1.15 size modifier; loads above 10,000 use 1.30.

Conventional war

Base Strength = Soldiers + 34 × Tanks + 85 × Ships + 62 × Fighter JetsEffective Strength = Base Strength × Operation × Posture × Readiness × Organization × Supply × Exhaustion × Intelligence × Round Variance

Round variance is 0.92–1.08. Aggressive posture is ×1.13; entrenched posture is ×1.08. Operations and objectives favor the unit types described in their interface. Drones use their separate mission system and are not included in conventional strength.

War result

A side wins immediately if enemy Resolve reaches zero. Otherwise the war ends after 20 three-hour rounds; a score lead greater than 3 decides the winner, while a margin of 3 or less is a stalemate. Infrastructure and institution damage is capped by the limits shown on the war screen.
